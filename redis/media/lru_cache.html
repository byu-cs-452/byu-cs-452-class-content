    Run some requests to compare.

    The dictionary jumps straight to a photo's node, so nothing gets searched. The doubly linked list moves that node to the front by changing a handful of arrows, whether the list holds 10 photos or 10 million. The back of the list is always the photo used longest ago, so throwing one out is instant too.

    A plain list has to look through the photos one by one to find the one you asked for, then slide everything behind it over to close the gap. That costs about a thousand steps every single time.
